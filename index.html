01 / PUBLIC DATAMalaysia · Labour market
OpenDOSM · Power BI

Malaysia Labour Market Intelligence

Explore employment trends and the composition of Malaysia's labour market in one interactive report.

Read project overview

Question. How is Malaysia's labour market changing over time?

Approach. Bring monthly DOSM labour force data into Power BI, organise key indicators, and compare trends and demographic breakdowns.

Selected finding. In June 2026, active unemployed persons represented 410.9k of the 517.8k total, or 79.35%.

Purpose. Make national labour statistics easier to explore. Figures describe the source data; they do not establish the causes of labour market changes.
