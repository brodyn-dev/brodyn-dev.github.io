MeBooks Reading Tracker

  • React
  • Supabase
  • Vercel

A reading tracker for logging books, reading sessions and streaks, with private shelves and a feed of what people you follow are reading.

Read project overview

Problem. Reading lists end up in notes apps or in your head, and most reading apps are public by default.

What it does. Add books by ISBN or manually, log pages read each session, see streaks and pages per year, and export your data to CSV.

Privacy. Private accounts and notes. Shelves can be shared with people you trust. The app’s About & privacy page explains what data is kept.

Status. Live web app. No personal or test account data is shown on this page.

D’Azi Vending Business Analytics

  • Operational reporting
  • Power BI

Reporting for a detergent vending business, connecting business performance with collection and refill activity.

Read project overview

Question. How can an owner monitor business performance and recurring vending operations in one place?

Scope. Executive reporting, sales by segment or region, and operational views of visits, refills and revenue by location and time.

Purpose. Support regular performance reviews and make operational records easier to interpret.

Privacy. Private business financial data and dashboards are not published on this website. No revenue improvement or cost saving is claimed.
